About

Architect and 3D visualiser with seven years of practice across Egypt and the Gulf. I design residential, coastal and corporate projects from concept to site, and I produce the exterior and interior renders, aerials and animated films that present them — for my own designs and for developers, studios and corporate clients. Doing both means my images are accurate and buildable, and my designs are presented at their best.
