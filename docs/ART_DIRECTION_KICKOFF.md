## 1. The brief for the new session
Build the **art direction** for the brand "Built as designed": one consistent visual language for photography and renders, drawings, layouts, social, print and motion. It has to work on the website, the portfolio PDFs, the CV, LinkedIn and Instagram.

**Expected outputs:**
1. An art-direction book: a mood and reference board, image rules, layout grid, type in use, colour in use, and do/don't pairs.
2. Templates for an Instagram post (4:5), a carousel, a story, a LinkedIn banner and post, a project sheet (A4 landscape) and a cover.
3. Render direction for future work: light, camera, entourage, grade and crops.
4. A one-page summary that Claude Code can apply to the site and PDFs.

## 2. Brand core (✅ decided, do not reopen)
- **Name / wordmark:** `AYA | HASSAN`, set in Archivo 800 at 125% width on one line, with a thin vertical divider.
- **Idea:** an idea only counts once it stands on real ground, from first sketch to a built place.
- **Motto:** *Bold on paper. Solid on ground.* · جريئة على الورق، راسخة على الأرض
- **Short line:** *Designed to be built.* · صُمّم ليُبنى
- **Positioning:** architect and interior designer for luxury homes, coastal developments and corporate HQs across Egypt and the Gulf. The coast leads.
- **Personality:** assured, precise, calm luxury, generous with credit.
- **Voice:** one sensory line, then one exact line. Restrained headlines, first person, numbers kept, no hype words outside developer marketing.
- **Six convictions:**
  1. Form and function in agreement.
  2. Buildable is part of beautiful.
  3. The route to yes.
  4. Honest images with atmosphere.
  5. Luxury is everything agreeing.
  6. On the coast, the site writes the brief.
- **Retired, do not bring back:** "One Hand", "From concept to conviction", the blue "cut open" concept, the approval stamp, gold, and the old AYA sprite logo.

## 4. Type (✅, with one open point)
- **Archivo** (variable, with a width axis) is the brand face.
  - Headlines: 800–900 weight, 112–125% width, tight tracking.
  - Accents: 200–300 italic.
  - Labels: 400–500 weight, uppercase, +0.18–0.24em tracking.
- **Arabic:** Alexandria.
- ⚠️ **Open point:** on 12 Nov Aya chose **Inter for body text in the portfolio PDFs**, keeping Archivo for titles. The site and brand rules still say one typeface. The new session should decide whether Inter becomes official for body text everywhere, or stays in the PDFs only.

## 5. Image system — "house look" (✅)
- **Renders:**
  - One warm grade: `sepia(.14) saturate(.86) contrast(1.05) brightness(1.01)`. This gives sand highlights, matte blacks and quieter greens and blues.
  - Cover pages use a night overlay that fades left to right, with the text on the right.
- **Drawings:**
  - Bone paper, ink linework and sand fills, with oxblood for key layers.
  - Each building's drawings share one scale and are combined onto as few sheets as possible.
- **Study boards:** keep their original colours. A recolour to the house palette was tried and rejected.
- **Future renders:**
  - Light: a "Cairo 5 pm" warm late afternoon.
  - Camera: about 1.2 m high, 35 mm lens for interiors, verticals corrected.
  - Entourage: neutral, slightly blurred people and one plant family.
  - Fine grain throughout.
  - Crops: 4:5 and 21:9.
- **Never use:** real-tower photos of STC or SABIC, or Misk Schools drawings (confidential).

## 6. Layout devices in use now
- **Kept:** hairline rules, dimension-line ruler (intro and hero), project numbers (01, 02…), uppercase labels, logo chips in their own colours on a bone background.
- **Removed 12 Nov:** sheet numbers (A-0xx) in the PDFs. They're gone from the PDFs, but §2.3 of the handover still lists them as a device. The new session should confirm whether they're retired everywhere.
- **Portfolio structure:**
  - Cover → About (CV text + employment list) → Clients → Index → Contents.
  - Sections: 01 Architecture, 02 Interiors, 03 BIM.
  - Each project: an opener (full-bleed render with overlay, number and title on the left, info on the right, client logo only), then studies, then plans, then renders.
